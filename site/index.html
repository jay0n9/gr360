GR360 explores how a single text prompt can become a 360° video with accompanying audio. Look in any direction and replay each five-second scene.

Overview

GR360 brings together text-to-360° video, spatial audio generation, and looping environments. Describe a scene to create a world of surrounding images and sound.

The demos bring together panoramic views, environmental motion, and sound in short, repeating clips. Explore a quiet coast, a forest waterfall, or a campfire at dusk directly in your browser.

Project Features

From a written scene to an environment you can explore.

Text to 360°

Turn a scene description into a panoramic video. Look around in every direction from one viewpoint.

Spatial Audio

Generate spatial audio for 360° scenes, placing sounds within the surrounding environment.

Looping scenes

Replay short moments of environmental motion and sound, with time to explore the surroundings.

Panoramic Outputs

The full equirectangular frames behind the interactive scenes.

About the Demos

These demos are a work in progress. Distortion, stretched textures, and repeated patterns can appear near the poles. Motion and appearance changes may remain at the loop boundary.

The current web demos play a stereo preview. They do not yet demonstrate spatial audio playback that changes with your viewing direction. Use the sound button to listen and drag the video to look around.
