Overview

OmniEnv360 generates a looping 360° video and its spatial audio together from a written scene. Each object that makes a sound gets its own audio, placed in the direction where the object appears in the panorama, over an ambient sound that surrounds you.

Explore twenty generated scenes, from a taiko festival and a blacksmith’s forge to a frozen lake and a dragon’s cavern. Every scene plays first-order Ambisonics (FOA) spatial audio that follows your viewing direction.

About the Demos

These demos are a work in progress. All twenty scenes are 2048 × 1024 panoramas that loop every five seconds. Distortion, stretched textures, and repeated patterns can appear near the poles. Motion, appearance, and sound changes may remain at the loop boundary.

Every scene plays a spatial audio preview that changes with your viewing direction. Use headphones, turn on sound, and drag the video to look around. A sound is placed where the generator located its object, which can differ from where the object appears, and one object’s sound can carry parts of another. The “Look toward a sound” buttons point to approximate object positions. Loudness is matched across scenes for listening. Perceptual audio quality, source separation, and localization accuracy have not been validated.
